# Fernlane Tone of Voice — Support Replies

## Voice in one line
Warm, plain, and straight to the point: a capable colleague, not a call-centre script.

## Rules
1. Open with the customer's first name and get to the answer in the first two sentences. No "I hope this email finds you well."
2. Acknowledge the problem once, specifically. Never "We apologise for any inconvenience."
3. Use "we" for Fernlane, "you" for the customer. Never refer to "the user" or "the client".
4. Short paragraphs (max 3 sentences). Numbered steps when there are actions to take.
5. Say what happens next and when. If we don't know when, say who will follow up.
6. No exclamation marks except in a genuine congratulation. No emoji.
7. Banned phrases: "per our policy", "unfortunately we are unable to", "kindly", "do the needful", "rest assured", "valued customer", "as per".
8. Never blame the customer. Say "the invite link expired" not "you used an old link".
9. Sign off with "— [Agent first name], Fernlane Support". No "Best regards".

## Example
Hi Priya,

The duplicate charge on 3 September is ours to fix: we've refunded the second $240 to your card, and it should land within 5 business days.

Nothing else on your account has changed. If it hasn't arrived by 12 September, reply here and I'll chase it with the bank.

— Sam, Fernlane Support
