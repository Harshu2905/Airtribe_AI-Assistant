# Fernlane Refund & Pricing Policy

Version: 2026-09-15 (supersedes 2026-07-01). Owner: Finance Ops.
This is the ONLY source of truth for figures quoted to customers.

## Plans and list prices (billed in USD)
- Starter: $12 per seat per month, or $120 per seat per year
- Team: $24 per seat per month, or $240 per seat per year
- Business: $45 per seat per month, or $450 per seat per year
- Enterprise: custom pricing. Never quote a number; route to Sales.

## Discounts
- Annual billing: equivalent to two months free (already reflected in annual prices above).
- Nonprofit / education discount: 30% off Team or Business, annual billing only, after verification.
- No other discounts may be offered by Support. Promotional codes are owned by Marketing.

## Refunds
- New subscriptions: full refund if requested within 14 days of the first charge.
- Annual plans after 14 days: prorated refund of unused full months, minus a $25 processing fee.
- Monthly plans after 14 days: no refund for the current month; cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period.
- Seat reductions: credited to the next invoice, not refunded to card.
- Service-outage credits: 10% of that month's subscription fee for any month where uptime falls below 99.5%. Customer must request within 30 days.
- Duplicate charges: always refunded in full, no fee, within 5 business days.

## Anything not listed
If a customer asks about a figure, plan, or exception not listed here, do not estimate.
Say the team will confirm and route to Billing.
